Account Data & Deletion

Submit a request to permanently delete your OpenVersus account and personal data. Requests are reviewed by an admin before processing — typically within a few days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (in-game name, identity tokens, blocklist)
  • Your ELO ratings, personal stats, and friend list
  • Your cosmetics / loadout records
  • Cached connection state on our servers

What's anonymized (kept for game integrity)

  • Match records you participated in are kept for fairness to the other players, but your name and account ID are replaced with deleted_user_xxxxxxxx and can no longer be associated with you.
  • Historical leaderboard entries are anonymized rather than removed — gaps would break rankings for everyone else.

Submit a request

Your IP address will be recorded with the request to help us identify the right account (multiple players can share an in-game name). It is not displayed publicly and is deleted along with your account.
